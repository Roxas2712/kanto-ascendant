# Kanto Ascendant 5.0.1 — Complete 5.0 Release

Kanto no longer ends at the Hall of Fame. Version 5.0 turns it into a living
251-Pokémon endgame built around discovery, collection and increasingly wild
level-100 challenges.

**New in the completed 5.0 release**

🧭 A spoiler-safe Research Atlas that reveals only Pokémon and habitats you
have actually discovered
📓 One shared Journal/Atlas quest tracker with the next objective, location
and exact progress
👨‍🔬 A one-time Oak onboarding for both new and upgraded post-game saves
🌿 44 permanent Living Johto habitats, making all 251 Pokémon obtainable in
one save
🏭 A level-100 Battle Factory with changing rental drafts
🚢 Repeatable five-battle S.S. Anne Grand Tours
🧠 A legal Move Reminder, lossless renewable-TM queue and Frontier Exchange
🏆 A Legacy Gallery with trophies and selectable Trainer Card titles
🥇 Gold as the final required main battle before New Game+, while the Factory
and S.S. Anne remain optional prestige challenges

**The complete Ascendant experience also includes**

⚔️ Step-based trainer rematches with stronger, growing teams and rare rewards
✨ real Gen-II shinies, outbreaks and a 251-species Shiny Dex
🥚 the Route 5 Day-Care, Eggs and Johto evolution research
🗿 official Mega Evolutions with individual Mega Stones
🌈 all 100 Johto species, legendary events, Master Leaders, Apex/Crown
challenges and the Silver/Kris/Gold finale
🎁 complete Kanto catchability, renewable TMs, starter moves, event Pokémon,
New Game+ and full English/German support

Rematches and major Ascendant battles award no prize money. Progress, titles,
Frontier Points and carefully gated item rewards are the prize.

The release was tested against historical 1.x, 2.x, 3.x and 4.x save schemas
across Red, Blue and Yellow, English and German, including restart and
mod-disable/re-enable paths. More features, dialogue and balancing will still
follow, and feature wishes remain very welcome.
