# Kanto Ascendant 5.0.2

Kanto no longer ends at the Hall of Fame. Ascendant turns it into a living
251-Pokémon endgame full of discovery, collection and level-100 challenges.

**New in the completed 5.0 release**

🧭 Spoiler-safe Research Atlas plus a shared quest tracker
👨‍🔬 Oak onboarding for new and upgraded post-game saves
🌿 44 Living Johto habitats — all 251 Pokémon in one save
🎨 Bundled normal/shiny Crystal battle and Gen-2 follower sprites
🏭 Level-100 Battle Factory with changing rental drafts
🚢 Repeatable five-battle S.S. Anne Grand Tours
🧠 Move Reminder, renewable-TM archive and Frontier Exchange
🏆 Legacy Gallery with trophies and selectable titles
🥇 Gold before the replayable Ascendant Cycle

**Also included**

⚔️ Step-based rematches with growing teams and rare rewards
✨ Gen-II shinies, outbreaks and a 251-species Shiny Dex
🥚 Route 5 Day-Care, Eggs and Johto evolution research
🗿 Official Mega Evolutions with individual Mega Stones
🌈 All Johto species, legendary events, Master Leaders, Apex/Crown challenges
and the Silver/Kris/Gold finale
🎁 Complete Kanto catchability, event Pokémon, renewable TMs, New Game+ and
full English/German support

Rematches and major Ascendant battles pay no prize money — progression,
titles, Frontier Points and item rewards are the prize.

Tested across historical 1.x–4.x saves, Red/Blue/Yellow, English/German,
restart and mod disable/re-enable paths.

More features and balancing will follow. Feedback and feature wishes are very
welcome!
