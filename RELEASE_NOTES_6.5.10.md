# Kanto Ascendant 6.5.10 — Rival, Leafeon and Raichu Hotfix

Kanto Ascendant 6.5.10 is a save-compatible gameplay and presentation hotfix
for Pokémon Red, Blue and Yellow. No new save or story reset is required.

## Yellow rival progression and Tower music

- When Red or Green is selected as Yellow's rival, all three Eevee-outcome
  party indices now resolve to the same intended story tier.
- The Squirtle line established on the S.S. Anne remains Wartortle in Pokémon
  Tower and becomes Blastoise later instead of changing into another starter.
- Winning the Pokémon Tower rival battle restores the room music immediately
  on engine builds that otherwise keep playing the rival theme.
- Blue's native teams and Red/Blue edition progression remain unchanged.

## Leafeon's Leaf Blade

- Leafeon now learns Leaf Blade at level 71.
- Leaf Blade remains a classic special Grass move for every other species.
- A species-scoped battle rule makes it physical only when Leafeon uses it.

## Yellow partner Raichu faces

- The Ascendant Raichu face set has been recropped to remove clipped tails and
  detached edge fragments.
- Yellow receives a RAICHU FACES option with corrected ASCENDANT and animated
  CLASSIC YELLOW styles.
- The default remains ASCENDANT. Pikachu, Gorochu and Red/Blue are unaffected.

## Included previous hotfix

Version 6.5.10 also contains 6.5.9's renewable Yellow Koffing and Weezing
encounters and the corrected Red/Blue/Yellow reachability audit.

## Install or update

1. Close the game and launcher.
2. Back up important saves.
3. Import `kanto_ascendant-6.5.10.zip` through the launcher.
4. Resolve every reported conflict, then restart the launcher and game.

The matching checksum file is `SHA256SUMS-6.5.10.txt`.
