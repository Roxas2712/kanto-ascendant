# Kanto Ascendant 5.2.3 — Kanto Backs & Wilds Compatibility

This hotfix closes the two remaining visual encounter gaps reported after
5.2.2:

- **KANTO CRYSTAL ART** now includes authentic normal and shiny player backs
  for all 151 Kanto Pokémon. Yellow's special Pikachu therefore uses its
  matching Crystal rear view at the correct native size.
- Turning Kanto Crystal art off still restores the original Red, Blue or
  Yellow front and back sprites.
- Researched Johto habitats now work with **Wilds of Kanto**. Eligible
  post-Hall-of-Fame Johto encounters can appear as visible overworld Pokémon
  at their authored map and level.
- All 100 Johto species receive stable Wilds renderer registrations, so their
  generated species art is used instead of a shared placeholder.
- **PokéPC Followers compatibility** no longer converts Yellow's opening
  level-5 Pikachu into Charmander. Oak catches Pikachu again and the original
  starter name and dialogue are restored without disabling follower choice.

The original Hall-of-Fame and Elm research progression is unchanged. Existing
Red, Blue and Yellow saves remain fully compatible. Import
`kanto-ascendant-5.2.3.zip` or the identical `.modpkg` through the launcher.
