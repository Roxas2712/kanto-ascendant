# Kanto Ascendant 5.2.1 — Johto Display & Audio Hotfix

This small compatibility update fixes the three issues reported shortly after
5.2.0:

- Johto Crystal back sprites now render at the correct 1× size.
- Gen-II fallback cries are rebound after save loading and stale failed audio
  lookups are cleared.
- PokéPC Followers keeps using Ascendant's species-accurate normal and shiny
  Johto sheets even when another follower or graphics wrapper is installed.
  Existing mobile follower caches are regenerated automatically.

Existing Red, Blue and Yellow saves remain compatible. Import
`kanto-ascendant-5.2.1.zip` or the identical `.modpkg` through the launcher.
