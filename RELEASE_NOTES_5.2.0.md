# Kanto Ascendant 5.2.0 — Complete Mega Art & Starter Relics

Kanto Ascendant 5.2.0 completes the visual Mega Evolution pass and adds three
guided Johto Starter Relic journeys.

## Highlights

- All 30 official Mega forms now ship with dedicated sharp 96×96 normal and
  shiny front/back art.
- Every form has side-aware Crystal-style animation for classic 2D and
  Dramatic Shape/Voxel battles.
- Individual 2D player-back anchors keep large wings, shoulders and heads out
  of the HP HUD. The original opaque command box masks the lower sprite area.
- Disabling Crystal art uses dedicated static four-shade Mega cards with the
  active Red, Blue or Yellow palette instead of ordinary species sprites.
- Owning any member of the Chikorita, Totodile or Cyndaquil family assigns a
  one-time bilingual Starter Relic journey, including upgraded saves, wild
  catches, gifts and boxed Pokémon.
- The Verdant and Torrent journeys award the otherwise unforgeable
  Meganiumite and Feraligatrite.
- After Gold, a complete 251 Pokédex and a level-100 Typhlosion can awaken the
  deliberately unofficial secret **Ascendant Typhlosion** at the Basalt Seal
  in Pokémon Mansion B1F.
- Ascendant Typhlosion has dedicated normal/shiny front and back animation,
  including a Crystal-derived cyan volcanic eruption.
- Ascendant and Journal menus show `NEW` markers and always name the next
  Starter Relic objective and location.
- External Gen-II cry packs now take priority automatically. Ascendant adds
  its own synthesized cry only when a Johto species still has no audio.

Existing Red, Blue and Yellow saves remain supported. Crystal Animated Sprites
with Shiny Visuals and Dramatic Shape remain optional integrations.

Download either `kanto-ascendant-5.2.0.zip` or the identical launcher package
`kanto-ascendant-5.2.0.modpkg`, then import it through the Gen1 Recomp launcher.
