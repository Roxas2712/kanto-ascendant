RC27 replaces the withdrawn RC26 archive after the first manual dungeon and
Elite Four test. It removes no requested gameplay system.

## Corrected after the RC26 field test

- Makes the three post-League wall fissures clearly readable and adds a
  character-specific draft prompt with a default **NO** before entry.
- Moves BLUE's Route 24 fissure from the foreground to the rear rock wall at
  `(10,3)`; its approach and return cell is `(10,4)`.
- Keeps RED, BLUE and GREEN trials strictly character-bound, including shrine
  entry, shared-room handoff and return to the matching exterior fissure.
- Restores opaque darkness and correct map music in the trial caves, including
  native save loads and floor changes.
- Activates the character-bound wild encounter tables while the examination
  is in progress. Parents start at level 70 and scale by Legacy cycle up to
  level 100; the shared tunnel and sealed antechamber remain encounter-free.
- Caps the visible trial Wilds population after both initial and later spawns.
- Treats every visible native CAVERN ladder in the sixteen HEVO maps as a real
  warp contract; decorative ladder graphics without a destination are invalid.
- Persists the final-stair character handoff before the common tablet is used,
  so the last-chance Mega Stone, matching legendary blackout and Oak call
  survive reload and cannot resolve as another character.
- Preserves the accepted dense RED, BLUE and GREEN puzzle routes, including
  their reset/recovery paths and five authored relic contacts per character.

## Presentation and postgame fixes retained in RC27

- Crystal-colour Pokémon presentation, including Oak's intro demo and the
  Hall of Fame; Gorochu has animated Crystal and FULL-Voxel presentation.
- FULL-Voxel Elite Four standees and the reviewed Crown roster without the
  reported duplicate legendary/species choices.
- The Johto Masters host, separate Silver/Kris/Gold battlefields and their
  generated G/S Indigo Plateau and Rival Battle themes.
- SELECT item help in Ascendant's Bag and repaired bedroom-PC run rules.
- Four Legacy pacts and four separately stored Bank policies, Oak-hosted
  confirmation, fresh-run Lab landing/exit lock, the Yellow one-ball path,
  and deterministic rival-counter assignment for the free partner catalog.
- Surprise trainers, expanded rematch rewards, Apricorn Balls and registered
  Gen-II/III TM rewards remain enabled.

## Test boundary

The current BLUE rear-wall art is accepted in native 2D and FULL Voxel. The
map/entry/return contract is covered by the current Authority and shared-tunnel
suite; a separate Blue-edition targeted launcher hung before its driver began
and is not represented as a new end-to-end runtime pass.

Do not continue testing with `kanto-ascendant-6.5.0-rc26-test.zip` after
installing RC27.
