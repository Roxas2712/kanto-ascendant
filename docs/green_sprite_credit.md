# Green sprite reference and credit

The production Green walker, player front and battle back in this Phase-8
demo are adapted from Felix Jones' Pokémon Green sprite work:

<https://felixjones.co.uk/2025/04/30/pokegreen.html>

Felix's article documents the 16×16 overworld poses, the 56×56 trainer art,
the 32×32 battle back, solid-block Gen-I hair shading, side-view Poké Ball
earring and hair highlights. Kanto Ascendant stacks the exact first two frames
of Felix's down/up/side walking GIFs in Gen1 Recomp's native six-frame order.
No hair, hand, silhouette or colour detail is added to the overworld frames;
the engine applies its normal Game Boy object palette. The separate 56×56
player/selector portrait is the exact final frame of Felix's `progress.gif`,
reduced only from its documented 3× nearest-neighbour display size. It is not
generated from, and is never substituted by, the 16×16 walking sheet.

The page does not state an asset licence. The demo therefore records the
source and credit in this file and in PNG metadata. Confirm permission with
Felix Jones before a public release that redistributes these pixels.
