# Kanto Ascendant 5.4.0 — The Lost Thunder

Raichu's lost final evolution has arrived as a complete guest species.

## Gorochu

- Raichu can permanently evolve into **Gorochu** in Red, Blue and Yellow.
- Requirement: enter the Hall of Fame, reach high bond, know **Thunder**, and
  level up inside the **Power Plant**.
- Yellow's original partner keeps its identity, happiness, memories and
  follower after evolving from Raichu into Gorochu.
- Gorochu has dedicated normal and shiny front/back art, six-frame Crystal
  battle animation and normal/shiny follower sheets.
- Yellow partner Gorochu has seven separate animated faces: sleepy, unwell,
  upset, wary, content, devoted and excited.
- Gorochu uses a safe Raichu-derived cry when no external cry is installed.
- The Research Atlas explains the evolution only after discovery.

Gorochu is registered as guest Pokédex entry **1026**. It never increases the
original 151/251 certificate or Shiny Charm requirements.

Existing Red, Blue and Yellow saves remain compatible.
