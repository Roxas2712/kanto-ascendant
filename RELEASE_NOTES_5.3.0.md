# Kanto Ascendant 5.3.0 — Heart of Thunder

Yellow's original partner finally gets to choose its own path.

After earning the Thunder Badge, speak to Lt. Surge to begin the optional
Heart of Thunder journey. Walk 251 steps and win three trainer battles with
your original partner, then return to receive the permanent Thunderheart.

- Evolve the partner into Raichu, stay Pikachu or decide later.
- Thunderheart can never be consumed, sold, discarded or deposited.
- Partner Raichu keeps Yellow's happiness, memories, bond moments and follower.
  Speaking to it can now show sleepy, unwell, upset, wary, content, devoted or
  excited reactions. Each has its own animated Crystal portrait, matching
  bubble and spoken Raichu clip in Yellow-style one-bit mono audio.
  The portrait automatically moves opposite the bubble. Sleepy and unwell use
  restrained custom eyelids; sleepy keeps Crystal's original yawn while
  unwell adds a small frown. The five moods Crystal already depicts well keep
  its official expressions.
  Battle sprites remain unchanged.
- A partner that stays Pikachu can later answer Raichunite X/Y directly,
  temporarily becoming the matching official Mega Raichu in battle.
- Existing Yellow saves that already received Oak's starter get Thunderheart
  automatically and skip the early trial.
- If an old save contains several self-owned Pikachu/Raichu, Thunderheart asks
  which one is the original partner instead of guessing.
- Red and Blue remain completely unaffected.

Existing saves remain compatible.
