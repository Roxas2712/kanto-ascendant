# Kanto Ascendant 5.0.0 — Development Preview

Kanto Ascendant 5.0 expands the persistent post-game into a complete
251-species discovery, training and collection loop. This release is fully
playable, but remains an active development preview: more dialogue, balancing,
features and polish will follow.

## Highlights

- **Research Atlas:** Oak's current objective, discovered habitats, recorded
  trainer ranks and cooldowns, reward gates, TM queues and encounter details
  in one spoiler-safe bilingual interface.
- **Living Johto:** 44 permanent thematic habitats unlock through research
  without pre-filling Pokédex flags. All 251 species are reachable in one
  save, including renewable evolution-item access.
- **Battle Factory:** draft three level-100 rentals from six candidates and
  clear three sealed-Bag rounds for Frontier Points. Original parties are
  restored atomically after every outcome.
- **S.S. Anne Grand Tour:** a repeatable five-battle level-100 voyage with
  mid-tour healing, Frontier Point rewards and a real-step departure timer.
- **Field economy:** a lossless FIFO TM archive, legal Move Reminder and
  Frontier Exchange for recorded TMs and released evolution items.
- **Legacy Gallery:** permanent trophy displays and selection of any unlocked
  achievement as the active Trainer Card title.

## The complete Ascendant endgame

- Ranked step-based rematches with visible cooldowns, silent background growth,
  class-themed party recruitment and level scaling to 100.
- Complete Kanto catchability, renewable Eevee, both fossils, starter rewards,
  trade-evolution alternatives and a separate authored Mew finale.
- All 100 Johto species, starter research trials, breeding, Eggs, friendship,
  day/night evolution and a Generation-II DV-based shiny system.
- Legendary events for Articuno, Zapdos, Moltres, Mewtwo, Raikou, Entei,
  Suicune, Lugia, Ho-Oh, Celebi and Mew.
- Master Leaders, Apex Elite Four, Crown Champion, Rocket Resurgence, Heritage
  events, Ascendant Frontier, Johto Masters and level-100 New Game Plus.
- Individual Mega Stones for supported official Mega Evolutions, plus
  bilingual English/German presentation and optional local Crystal battle and
  follower-art installers.

## Balance and reliability

- Rematches and major Ascendant battles pay no prize money; reward tables,
  eligibility gates and no-drop odds are now authoritative and visible.
- Training Rush affects trainer recovery only. Eggs, friendship, world events,
  outbreaks and voyages always use literal walked tiles.
- Factory parties, queued TMs, Crown signatures and Frontier purchases recover
  safely from full Bags, interrupted runs and earlier preview saves.
- Four ROM-free regression suites pass **2,871 checks**, and the standalone
  reachability audit confirms **251/251 obtainable species**, 580 real
  encounter slots, 122 evolution edges, 44 research habitats and five
  renewable evolution-item paths.

## Installation

Download `kanto-ascendant-5.0.0.zip` below and import it through the Gen1
Recomp launcher. The identical `.modpkg` is included as an alternative for
launcher versions that use that extension. Existing Kanto Ascendant saves are
supported. Do not enable a second mod that adds a complete Johto species
roster at the same time.
