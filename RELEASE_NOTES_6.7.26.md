# KASC 6.7.26 — Habitat music and Hunting Club

Complete update based on 6.7.25. Recommended pairing: VASC 3.0.49. Published as a regular Latest release for the launcher; public test status remains unchanged.

## Changes
- Give all twelve starter habitats music, using three original themes for plant, fire and water habitats. Battle music takes over normally and the habitat theme returns afterwards.
- Simplify the Hunting Club board to contracts and their Club Point values. The selected contract shows its level and move requirements. Accepting, pausing and showing a partner refresh the menu immediately.
- Hide future and unaffordable rewards. Milestone gifts keep their contents secret until collected.
- Give Mira a short in-world introduction until the first accepted contract, then rotating brief remarks. Existing accepted, paused and completed contracts are recognized on older saves.
- Give Mira her own native sprite. With VASC 3.0.49, matching HD and voxel artwork follows the selected character style.

## Validation
4,402 contract checks and menu/dialogue regressions passed. Native Red checks cover music playback in all twelve habitats, battle takeover and return, route exit, Mira's style switching and four facings, and the dialogue/accept/pause/party/cancel flow. Package Lua syntax and archive checks are documented in QA-REPORT.md. No new physical phone test or full playthrough is claimed.

## Install
Close the game, update through the launcher or import Kanto-Ascendant-6.7.26.zip, and restart. Keep existing saves and optional/custom artwork. Update VASC to 3.0.49 for Mira's HD/voxel art and the Crystal-based battle-size correction. No ROM, engine or player save is included.
